HOW IT WORKS

Boats in. Fees collected. Repeat.

The whole harbor runs on one simple loop, and the agent never clocks out.

01

A trade sails in

Every buy or sell of $HARBOR is a boat arriving at the harbor.

02

It pays the dock fee

Trades on pump.fun generate creator fees. In the harbor, that's the dock fee.

03

The agent collects

The harbor agent greets each boat, collects the fee and stacks it in the harbor vault.

04

The log is public

Harbor reports go out on X, and the fee wallet is public so anyone can check it.
